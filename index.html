About me:
I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Engineering and the Department of Economics and Management at the University of Luxembourg. My research focuses on optimization under uncertainty and data-driven decision-making, with applications in economics, policy, mechanism design, and control. My research work has appeared in leading journals including Management Science, Operations Research, Mathematics of Operations Research, and Mathematical Programming, and has been recognized with multiple awards and recognitions. I currently serve as an Associate Editor for Management Science and Operations Research.

Prior to joining the University of Luxembourg, I received a Ph.D. in Management of Technology from École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) in 2020, where I worked with Daniel Kuhn. I hold a B.Sc. degree and an M.Sc. degree in Industrial Engineering from Bilkent University.

Key areas of interest:
Optimization under uncertainty: (distributionally) robust optimization, data-driven optimization, stochastic and dynamic programming, robust control
Policy and mechanism design: resource allocation problems, auctions, pricing, policy learning, societal problems, fairness, equity
